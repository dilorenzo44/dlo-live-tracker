DLo Live Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

DLo Live Tracker is a Chrome extension that provides live fantasy sports and sports bet tracking features.

Information the extension accesses

DLo Live Tracker may read fantasy sports and betting information displayed on supported websites when necessary to provide its tracking features.

How information is used

This information is used only to provide extension functionality, including displaying tracked bets, fantasy matchups, scores, player updates, and related alerts.

Local storage

Tracker information, preferences, and settings may be stored locally in the user's browser so the extension can preserve its state between pages and browser sessions.

Data sharing

DLo Live Tracker does not sell user data. The developer does not use collected website content for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Authentication and financial information

DLo Live Tracker does not collect passwords, authentication credentials, credit card numbers, bank account information, or payment credentials.

Third-party services

The extension may retrieve sports data from third-party sports data services, including ESPN, in order to provide live scores, statistics, and game information.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be submitted through the DLo Live Tracker GitHub repository.
